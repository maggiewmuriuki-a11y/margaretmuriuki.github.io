About Me

I make complex technology easier for people to use.

My background sits at the intersection of technology, customer support and operations. I have spent my career helping people navigate systems, resolving problems, documenting solutions and improving the processes behind the service.

I enjoy getting to the bottom of how people actually work — what they are trying to achieve, where a process breaks down, what exceptions occur, and what can be simplified or automated.

More recently, I have been exploring AI tools and workflow automation to find practical ways of turning repetitive work into clearer, more efficient processes. I am particularly interested in helping non-technical users get real value from new technology without needing to understand everything happening behind the scenes.

How I Work

From messy process to something people can use.

Whether I am onboarding a user, solving a recurring support problem or exploring an automation opportunity, I start by understanding how the work actually happens.

01
Understand
Listen to the user and understand the goal, pain points and current process.
02
Map
Break the process into steps, decisions, exceptions and handoffs.
03
Build
Create a practical workflow, checklist, guide or automation that solves the problem.
04
Test
Try the process from the user's perspective and identify what could go wrong.
05
Document
Turn the solution into clear documentation others can follow without hand-holding.
06
Improve
Use feedback and recurring issues to make the process simpler and more effective.
